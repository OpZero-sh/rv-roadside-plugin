# Call brief

What the user should have ready before calling a shop, and the question that decides whether a shop can help. Pick the lines that apply. Do not send the whole list.

## Have ready

**About the rig**

- type: motorhome (Class A, B or C), travel trailer, fifth wheel, truck camper, van
- year, make and model
- overall length, and height if a shop bay or a tow is involved
- weight rating from the weight label (GVWR)
- for a motorhome: the chassis make, and the engine if known
- VIN, for parts and warranty questions

**About the problem**

- what happened, in order, and what the rig is doing now
- any warning lights or messages, word for word
- whether it can be driven or towed safely
- for an appliance or component: brand, model number and serial number from its data plate
- for tires: the full size and load range from the sidewall, the position of the failed tire, and whether there is a usable spare
- whether the rig is under a warranty or a service contract, and with whom

**About where it is**

- the nearest town and the road, with a mile marker or exit if on a highway
- whether it is on pavement, a shoulder, a campsite or soft ground
- any limits on access: low clearance, narrow roads, gates, weight limits

## The deciding question, by need

| Need | Ask the shop |
| --- | --- |
| Motorhome chassis or engine | "Do you service a [chassis make] motorhome chassis, and can you take a vehicle [length] feet long?" |
| House systems | "Do you work on [system] for a [make and model], and how soon can you look at it?" |
| Appliance | "Do you service [brand and model], and do you carry or can you get parts for it?" |
| Mobile service | "Do you come out to [town or campground], and what is the call out charge?" |
| Tires | "Do you have [size and load range] in stock, and can you mount it on a [rig type] on site or at your shop?" |
| Towing | "Can you tow a [rig type] that is [length] feet and about [weight] pounds, and where would you take it?" |
| Trailer running gear | "Do you repack bearings and service brakes on a [number] axle trailer of this weight?" |

## Also worth asking

- hours today, and whether they take emergency or after hours calls
- the diagnostic or call out fee before work starts
- the earliest appointment
- whether the user can stay in the rig while it is at the shop, if that matters

## For the assistant

- Offer one deciding question for each shop, fitted to that shop's listing.
- Use the user's own numbers. If a number is missing, tell the user where to find it, not what it probably is.
- Do not quote prices, wait times or capabilities that are not in a record.
